About Him

A Father, A Teacher, A Role Model

Alhaji Ibrahim Usman is an Islamic Scholar and Teacher who has a deep interest in Islamic knowledge and the well being of the people around him.

He is a father who cares deeply about his children and wants them to pursue education and become knowledgeable individuals.

He is also someone who values peace, unity, kindness, and good relationships among people.

To me, he is more than a father. He is my teacher, my guide, and someone I am always proud of.

With respect and love.
